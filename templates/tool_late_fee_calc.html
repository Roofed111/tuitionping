How the late-fee calculation works

Start with unpaid tuition = original tuition − prior payment or credit. Then calculate billable days = days overdue − grace days, with a minimum of zero. Fees begin only after the grace period and only if tuition remains unpaid.

Formulas for the five supported late fee types
Fee modelFormula after grace ends
One-time flatFlat fee once
DailyDaily rate × billable days
Per started weekWeekly rate × billable days rounded up to groups of 7
One-time percentageUnpaid tuition × percentage ÷ 100
Flat + dailyFlat fee + daily rate × billable days

A cap limits only the late fee, not tuition. Percentage fees round to the nearest cent; fees do not compound.

Worked example: $1,200 tuition, 7 days late

With a $200 prior credit, a 2-day grace period, a $5 daily rate and a $20 cap: unpaid tuition is $1,000, billable days are 5, and the uncapped fee is $25. The cap reduces the fee to $20. Total remaining due: $1,020.

Frequently asked questions

Does a grace period erase the earlier overdue days?

In this calculator, grace days are excluded from the fee calculation. Seven days overdue with two grace days means five billable days. Some agreements assess fees retroactively after grace expires; this tool does not model that rule.

How are weekly late fees counted?

A started week is any group of up to seven billable calendar days. Days 1–7 count as one week, 8–14 as two. The calculation begins after grace ends.

Can I calculate a fee on a partially paid bill?

Yes, when the payment or credit occurred before late fees began. The tool subtracts it from tuition, and percentage fees apply to the remaining balance. Payments made after fees started require a dated payment history and are not modeled here.

Is this a late pickup fee calculator?

No. This tool calculates late tuition payments by day. Late pickup charges based on minutes after closing use a different policy and calculation.

What if tuition is fully paid or payment is not overdue?

The estimated new late fee is zero when the remaining tuition balance is zero, payment is on or before the due date, or the grace period has not ended. Fees already accrued before a payment are outside this estimate.

Do I need an account, and are my inputs saved?

No account is needed. Calculations run in your browser; this tool does not submit your inputs to TuitionPing or save them in an account. The site’s normal visit analytics still apply. Copying a calculation link puts its inputs in the URL, where recipients can see them.

Will this update my provider account’s fee policy?

No. This is a standalone planning tool. Its five models may differ from the fee options available in your TuitionPing account.
