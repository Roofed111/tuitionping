The service

TuitionPing sends automated tuition-reminder text messages on your behalf to phone numbers you provide. You are responsible for ensuring you have consent to text the numbers you add — for example, by collecting consent at enrollment or in your tuition agreement.

SMS Terms

TuitionPing ("the SMS program") sends automated tuition-reminder text messages on behalf of your daycare provider. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies: typically up to 4 reminders per billing period, plus replies to requests you make (such as balance checks). Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out. To resubscribe after opting out, reply START. For questions about these SMS terms, contact our support team. For how messaging data is handled, see our Privacy Policy and SMS Privacy Policy. Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Customer referral credits

Use a valid customer referral code when creating your account. After the referred provider completes their first full paid month of TuitionPing service, both accounts receive subscription account credit equal to one month at their respective plan’s monthly list price when the credit is issued. The free trial, zero-dollar invoices and partial-month plan adjustments do not qualify. Annual subscriptions qualify after their first calendar month of paid service. A payment refunded, credited back or disputed before qualification, or service ending before that month is complete, does not qualify.

Credits are processed automatically, usually within an hour of eligibility, and apply to future TuitionPing subscription invoices. Processing failures remain pending for retry. Unused credit carries forward; credit is not a cash payment or card refund. Each referred account can earn one referral reward; self-referrals do not qualify. Credits issued under the previous referral offer remain on the account.

You agree to only add phone numbers of people who have agreed to receive tuition reminders, and to keep family information accurate and up to date. You will not use the service to send marketing messages or messages unrelated to tuition billing.

Billing

Every plan includes a 30-day free trial. A card is collected at signup but you are not charged until the trial ends. You can cancel anytime from the Billing page; cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period and reminders stop then.
