Before you add this to your enrollment packet

  1. State the due date and cutoff. Include a time and timezone if your policy uses one.
  2. Define grace precisely. Say whether calendar days count, whether fees begin after grace or apply retroactively, and how weekends are handled.
  3. Name the fee trigger. One time per bill, daily, and recurring charges are different policies. Do not mix them without explaining each.
  4. Describe credits and payments. Keep dates and amounts so a partial payment can be reconciled correctly.
  5. Document arrangements. Record any waiver or payment plan and apply your stated exception process consistently.
  6. Disclose changes before using them. Follow the notice and acknowledgment process in your agreement.

Common policy questions

Does a five-day grace period mean the fee starts on the fifth?

In this template, no. Five full calendar days follow the due date; the fee begins on overdue day six. If your agreement uses another rule, spell it out and check your examples.

Should unpaid fees repeat every month?

This template assesses one fee for one bill. Repeating the same fee on an older bill is a separate rule and is not implied by a new month's tuition becoming due.

Can I use this for a late pickup?

This is a tuition-payment policy. Charges for minutes after closing need a separate pickup policy.

How do I follow up without a difficult pickup conversation?

Use a private message with the due date, actual remaining balance, and payment method. The English and Spanish reminder templates include before-due and overdue examples. Record your follow-up in the free payment tracker.
