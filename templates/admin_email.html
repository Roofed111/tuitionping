Automated setup follow-ups

  • After 24 hours: unfinished checkout, for verified accounts created within the past seven days.
  • After 24 hours of a trial: no families added.
  • After three days of a trial: families added but no scheduled reminder recorded. A future due date may be expected.
  • Within three days of trial end: billing review and cancellation information.

Each stage is sent at most once per account, with at least 48 hours between setup guidance emails. Progress is checked again before sending. Active, canceled, expired, suspended or unverified accounts are excluded, including scheduled trial cancellations. Live trial billing is refreshed before guidance; if it cannot be verified, guidance waits. Opt-outs stop the follow-ups. The existing hourly scheduler processes up to 20 queued emails per run.
