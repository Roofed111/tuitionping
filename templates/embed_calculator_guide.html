What does it calculate?

Payments or credits entered here are applied before fees accrue. Fees use calendar days after the grace period, with no compounding. Weekly fees apply to each started group of seven billable days. The fee cap limits the fee, rather than the original tuition balance.

This is an estimate from the terms the provider enters. The calculator does not assess charges, send messages, collect money or determine whether a fee is permitted. Providers should apply their written agreement and check applicable requirements.

How does partner measurement work?

Loading the widget creates no acquisition visitor or conversion event. When someone opens TuitionPing through a widget link, the normal first-party tracking and privacy preferences apply. A registered partner code can carry through to a later signup and paid subscription under the existing partner terms. Browser privacy settings may limit attribution.

Can this expose my website's visitor information?

The calculator does not read the parent page, send entered amounts, use browser storage, or send information to the embedding website. It loads its public HTML, scripts and styles from TuitionPing; ordinary hosting requests are still needed to deliver those files.
