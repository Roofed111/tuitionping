brightwheel: the closest like-for-like swap

An all-in-one platform combining recurring invoices, online payments and autopay with attendance, enrollment and program reporting, plus billing notifications for new, due and past-due balances. Choose it when you want Procare's category with a widely adopted, modern platform. Pricing is a personalized quote; confirm payment service fees.

Kangarootime: automation for growing groups

An all-in-one aimed at centers and multi-site operators: automated billing with payment drafts, contactless check-in, staff scheduling, ratio tracking and subsidy tools, with reporting across locations. Choose it when consistency and automation across sites is the point. Pricing is custom, by quote.

Famly: the parent-experience pick

An all-in-one platform covering billing with automated payments and invoicing, attendance, enrollment and a parent newsfeed with messaging translated across many languages. Founded in Denmark and growing in the US. Choose it when the daily parent-app experience drives your decision. Subscription pricing by plan; confirm current numbers with Famly.

Lillio: daily reports and family engagement

An all-in-one childcare app (formerly HiMama) best known for daily reports, real-time photos and videos, and family engagement, alongside attendance, check-ins, registration and payment options, and curriculum planning. Choose it when classroom-to-parent communication is the gap in your current setup.

Common questions

What is the closest alternative to Procare?

brightwheel is the closest like-for-like swap: an all-in-one platform with billing, online payments, attendance and parent communication. Kangarootime is the closest match when multi-site automation is the priority, and Famly when the parent-app experience matters most.

Why do programs look for Procare alternatives?

Procare has served childcare for over 30 years, so most comparisons are not about capability. Programs reassess when they want a different parent-app experience, prices published before a sales process, automation aimed at multiple sites, or a narrower tool that only fixes tuition follow-up.

Do Procare alternatives include billing and payments?

The all-in-one platforms on this page do: brightwheel, Kangarootime, Famly and Lillio each offer billing with some form of online payment. TuitionPing deliberately does not — it sends reminder texts only, and you keep collecting tuition the way you do now.

Can I switch away from Procare mid-year?

Programs do switch mid-year, but plan it: export your family, billing and attendance records first, ask the new provider exactly what their onboarding migrates, and run both systems for one billing cycle if you can. Procare's own pricing page describes migration help for programs moving to Procare; expect to ask each platform the same questions on the way in.

What does TuitionPing do that Procare does not?

A narrower job, done a specific way: scheduled tuition reminder texts that reach parents on any phone — no parent portal or app — with a PAID reply you verify against your own records, at published flat prices. It is not a Procare replacement; it covers the follow-up slice only.
