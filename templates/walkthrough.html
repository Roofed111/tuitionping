Where does this fit in your tuition process?

Use an invoice to state the bill, a past-due letter for a written overdue follow-up, and a receipt only for money you have verified. TuitionPing adds scheduled monthly reminder texts alongside your records; it is not a payment processor or a full childcare management platform.
