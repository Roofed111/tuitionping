Questions before you start

When does the subscription charge begin?

The {{ trial_days }}-day trial requires a card at secure checkout. There is no subscription charge during the trial. Your chosen monthly or annual plan starts afterward unless you cancel. Review the selected plan, cycle and charge at checkout.

Is annual pricing a monthly payment?

No. Annual billing charges the full yearly price after the trial: {% for key, plan in plans.items() %}{{ plan.name }} ${{ plan.price * 10 }}{{ ", " if not loop.last else "." }}{% endfor %} Monthly billing charges the displayed monthly amount instead.

Are the limits per child or per family?

The plan limit is based on family records across your account. Micro and Starter each support one location. Growth and Multi-site support unlimited locations within their total family capacity.

Do parents need to install an app?

No. Parents receive SMS messages and can reply PAID from their existing text-message app. Obtain permission before adding a parent's number; parents can reply STOP to opt out.

Can I keep accepting checks, cash or transfers?

Yes. Keep the method agreed with your families, including a payment service you already use. TuitionPing sends reminders and records payment reports; it does not process tuition or reconcile your bank account.

What if I want a free tool first?

Generate a payment reminder, download the payment tracker, or create an invoice or verified-payment receipt. These standalone resources work without a paid subscription.

How do I cancel?

Cancel from your account's Billing page. During the trial, cancel before it ends to avoid the subscription charge. For a paid subscription, cancellation takes effect at the end of its billing period.
