Quick answers

Reminders not going out? Make sure the family's due date is set and the phone number is correct — one wrong digit is the most common cause.

Parent says they didn't get a text? Ask them to reply START to your TuitionPing number, then you can resend.

A parent wants to stop texts? They can reply STOP anytime — we honor it instantly. You can also toggle them off on your dashboard.
