Before you send a reminder

Include your program name, the amount, the due date, and how to pay. Replace every placeholder, including the dollar sign if your program uses another currency. Send messages privately, with permission, and respect opt-outs.

Use the remaining balance for overdue messages, rather than the original tuition if a payment or credit has reduced it. A short text is easier to read; accented characters and message length can affect SMS segmentation.

Need to work out the amount still owed? The free daycare tuition payment tracker keeps bills, credits, verified payments and follow-up notes together in Excel or Google Sheets. Check the balance before copying it into a message.

What if a parent says they already paid?

Acknowledge the report, check your payment records, and resolve any mismatch privately. Do not send a receipt before verifying the amount. In TuitionPing, PAID is the reply keyword in either language and the dashboard distinguishes reported payments from provider confirmation.

When should a fee appear in a text?

After you have assessed it under your written policy. Avoid threatening a future fee that already applies. Use the late-fee calculator for planning and your own ledger for the actual balance.
