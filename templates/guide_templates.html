Daycare tuition reminder text templates

Copy, paste, customize. Four messages that cover the whole billing cycle — in English and Spanish. Free to use, no signup needed.

What makes a reminder text work

Every template below follows the same formula: program name (so they know it's you) + amount + due date + how to pay. Leave any of those out and you get a reply asking for it instead of a payment. Keep it under 160 characters when you can — one text segment, no split messages.

3 days before — the heads-up

This one prevents most late payments. Friendly, no pressure.

Template

Hi {Parent}, friendly reminder that tuition of ${Amount} for {Program} is due on {Date}. Pay here: {PayLink} — Thanks!

Hola {Parent}, recordatorio amistoso: la colegiatura de ${Amount} para {Program} vence el {Date}. Pague aquí: {PayLink} — ¡Gracias!

Due date — short and direct

Same-day reminders catch the "oh right, today!" parents. Keep it brief.

Template

Hi {Parent}, just a reminder that tuition of ${Amount} for {Program} is due today ({Date}). {PayLink}

Hola {Parent}, recordatorio: la colegiatura de ${Amount} para {Program} vence hoy ({Date}). {PayLink}

+3 days late — the gentle nudge

Only send to families who haven't paid. Assume forgetfulness, not defiance.

Template

Hi {Parent}, we haven't received tuition of ${Amount} for {Program} yet (was due {Date}). Reply PAID once sent, or pay here: {PayLink}

Hola {Parent}, aún no recibimos la colegiatura de ${Amount} para {Program} (vencía el {Date}). Responda PAID cuando pague, o pague aquí: {PayLink}

+7 days late — the firm follow-up

Direct, with the consequence stated. No anger, no apology.

Template

Hi {Parent}, tuition of ${Amount} for {Program} is now 7 days overdue. Please remit right away to avoid a ${LateFee} late fee. {PayLink}

Hola {Parent}, la colegiatura de ${Amount} para {Program} tiene 7 días de retraso. Pague lo antes posible para evitar un recargo de ${LateFee}. {PayLink}

Payment received — close the loop

Always confirm. It ends the mental thread and builds the pay → acknowledged habit.

Template

Thanks {Parent}! Receipt: ${Amount} for {Month} received. 🎉

¡Gracias {Parent}! Recibo: ${Amount} por {Month} recibido. 🎉

Three rules for all of them

1. Always include your program name. Parents get texts from many numbers. "TuitionPing:" or your program name in the first line prevents "who is this?"

2. One idea per text. Reminder texts aren't the place for policy updates or newsletter content. Amount, date, pay link, done.

3. Respect opt-outs instantly. If a parent replies STOP, stop texting them — immediately, no exceptions. It's federal law (TCPA), and it's also just decent. They can rejoin with START.
