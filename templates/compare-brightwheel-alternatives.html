Procare: the long-running all-in-one

Enrollment, attendance, staff scheduling, parent communication, compliance reporting and integrated billing (Tuition Express, with next-day funding) in one system, serving childcare for over 30 years across 40,000+ programs, per Procare. Choose it when you want a full platform with a long track record. Pricing is a flat subscription by quote, with no per-child fee.

Compare TuitionPing and Procare

Lillio: daily reports and family engagement

An all-in-one childcare app (formerly HiMama) best known for daily reports, real-time photos and videos, and family engagement, alongside attendance, check-ins, registration and payment options, and curriculum planning. Choose it when the classroom-to-parent daily experience is the priority.

Famly: a modern parent-app platform

An all-in-one platform covering billing with automated payments and invoicing, attendance, enrollment and a parent newsfeed with messaging translated across many languages. Founded in Denmark and growing in the US. Choose it when you want a polished parent app and automatic billing in one place. Subscription pricing by plan; confirm current numbers with Famly.

Kangarootime: automation for centers and groups

An all-in-one aimed at centers and multi-site operators: automated billing with payment drafts, contactless check-in, staff scheduling, ratio tracking and subsidy tools. Choose it when you run or are growing into multiple locations and want operations automated across sites. Pricing is custom, by quote.

Common questions

What is the closest alternative to brightwheel?

Other all-in-one childcare platforms: Procare, Lillio and Famly each combine billing with attendance and parent communication. Which is closest depends on which brightwheel features you actually use — billing and payments, daily reports, or enrollment and attendance.

Why do daycares look for brightwheel alternatives?

Needs differ. Some programs use only part of a full platform and want a narrower, cheaper scope. Others want prices published before a sales conversation, or a different parent experience than an app-based portal. Comparing alternatives is mostly about matching the tool's scope to the job you actually have.

Is there a free alternative to brightwheel?

No full childcare platform is free. Free tools cover pieces of the work — a payment tracker spreadsheet, an invoice generator, reminder templates. TuitionPing offers a 30-day free trial of its reminder subscription; the all-in-one platforms generally offer demos or trials you can confirm with each provider.

Can I add TuitionPing without leaving brightwheel?

Yes. TuitionPing is reminders only: scheduled tuition texts with a PAID reply you verify in your own records. It can run alongside brightwheel or any other system. The two do not sync, so keep one system as your ledger of record.
