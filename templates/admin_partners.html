How credit is assigned

The latest registered partner landing in the same browser within 30 days before signup gets credit for that new account. A second partner visit after signup does not move that account's credit. First-touch acquisition remains separate in Conversions.

These associations do not prove a partner caused a sale. Different browsers, blocked cookies, shared devices and opt-outs can affect counts. Known bots, logged-in browsing, Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control are excluded. History and browser/account links expire after 90 days. First reminder means provider acceptance by Twilio, not confirmed delivery. Paid follows the existing live, positive subscription-invoice checks.
