Partner earnings — get paid for paid subscriptions

Registered partners choose how they earn when a provider who arrived through their link becomes a paying TuitionPing customer:

  • Revenue share: 30% of everything the customer pays during their first 12 months (for example, $68.40 over a year on the $19/mo Starter plan, $140.40 on the $39/mo Growth plan).
  • Flat bounty: 3× the plan's monthly price (Micro $27 · Starter $57 · Growth $117 · Multi-site $177), paid after the customer's second paid invoice. Annual plans earn 30% of the annual payment, paid 45 days after purchase.

Partners receive a monthly report and payout by the 15th for the prior month (payouts under $25 roll forward). Commissions reverse on refunds or chargebacks, and partners are asked to disclose the relationship when sharing and to avoid spam or claims TuitionPing does not make — TuitionPing sends reminders and records PAID replies; it does not process tuition payments.
