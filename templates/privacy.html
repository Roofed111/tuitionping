Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

Your account: name, email, company name, and a salted hash of your password (never the password itself). Payment details are processed by Stripe and never touch our servers.

Family information you enter: parent names, phone numbers, tuition amounts, due dates, classroom or location, and each family's preferred language (English or Spanish). Optionally: a child's first name and birthday (for birthday texts), and absence records from parent replies.

Message records: every reminder we send and every reply we receive, with timestamps, delivery status, and opt-out status — so you can see exactly what was communicated.

Consent records: when you add a family, we record your attestation (who added them and when). The underlying proof of consent — the signed form or the verbal-consent record — is kept by you, the provider.

Program settings: location names, payment links you add, late-fee rules, and your message templates.

Campaign measurement: our existing public-page visit logs use a hashed IP address, page, timestamp, referring site and browser type. Conversion measurement also uses a random identifier in a signed first-party cookie that expires after 30 days. It records the first landing page, public campaign labels, product walkthrough plays and completions, demo interactions, successful resource-download requests and accepted setup-help requests (without form content). If you create an account, we associate that identifier with the account's signup, checkout, trial, first real scheduled tuition reminder and first positive subscription payment. Conversion events do not include names, email addresses, phone numbers, message content or arbitrary URL query values. Conversion history and browser-to-account links expire after 90 days. We exclude logged-in browsing and honor Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control for conversion measurement. No advertising trackers are used.

Setup-help requests: your name, email, optional program name, family-count range, account stage, help topic, optional note, contact permission and timestamps are saved in a private administrator inbox. We use them to respond to your request. Request notifications are emailed to TuitionPing support through Resend, with delivery status and private follow-up notes retained for support. A setup request does not create an account or subscribe you to marketing. Do not submit family contact details, passwords or financial records. You may ask support to delete your request. Hashed request-limit records expire after two days.

How we use it

We use your data to operate TuitionPing: sending tuition reminders by SMS, managing your account and billing, and providing support. We also deliver resources you request and send optional account setup guidance. If you explicitly request marketing and confirm your email, we send occasional tuition tips, product improvements and offers. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers. Phone numbers you enter are used solely to send the reminders you configure.

Email list and preferences: resource requests store your email, optional name, request source, timestamps and delivery status. Marketing consent records include the wording/version, request time and confirmation time. An account or resource request alone does not enroll you in marketing. Marketing subscriptions require confirmation by email. We use Resend to deliver email. Optional follow-ups and campaigns include an unsubscribe link that does not require an account. Opting out stops optional marketing and setup guidance, while necessary security and billing notices continue. Email opt-out does not cancel your subscription or change parent SMS permission.

Email contacts, consent and delivery records are retained to honor preferences and troubleshoot delivery; you can request deletion through support. After an opt-out, we retain a suppression record so the address is not accidentally added to future campaigns. Pending email confirmations expire after seven days. Short-lived hashed request-limit records expire after two days. Administrators can view the list and export only confirmed, eligible marketing subscribers.

Mobile messaging data. Mobile phone numbers and SMS opt-in and consent records are never sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates. They are disclosed only to our operational processors as needed to deliver the service: Twilio (message delivery) and Stripe (billing).

SMS and messaging

Adding a family does not create consent. You must obtain each parent's permission to receive tuition texts before adding their number — at enrollment, verbally, or in your tuition agreement (see our model consent language). When you add a family in TuitionPing you attest that you have that permission; you retain the underlying proof. Anyone can opt out at any time by replying STOP; opt-outs are honored immediately and the number will not be texted again unless they reply START.

Data security

Passwords are stored as salted hashes, never in plain text. Payment details are processed by Stripe and never touch our servers. Account access is protected by login sessions; contact us immediately if you believe your account was accessed without permission.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. If you cancel, you can request deletion of your account data via support; message logs needed for compliance disputes may be retained as required by law.

Questions

Contact us at contact our support team.

Free documents and partner links

The invoice and receipt generator works in your browser tab. Document fields and payment details are not sent to TuitionPing, saved in URLs, or automatically stored. Anonymous document-type events may be recorded under our existing acquisition measurement rules.

Registered partner links may associate a new account with the last partner resource visit in the same browser within 30 days before signup. Reporting shows aggregate visits and subscription milestones. First-touch attribution remains separate. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control exclusions apply, and browser history and account attribution links expire after 90 days.
