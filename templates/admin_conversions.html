What these numbers mean

  • First-touch source is kept for 30 days in a signed first-party cookie. Campaign labels use utm_source, utm_medium and utm_campaign.
  • Downloads count successful file requests, not whether a person opened the file.
  • Checkout and trial completion come from live Stripe confirmations. Paid means a positive subscription invoice confirmed by Stripe, including invoices marked paid outside Stripe.
  • First tuition reminder means Twilio accepted a real scheduled tuition message; it does not establish delivery or payment.
  • Known bots, logged-in browsing, Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control are excluded. Other bots, shared devices and blocked cookies can affect estimates.
  • Collection begins with this release. Earlier accounts are not backfilled. History and browser-to-account links expire after 90 days.
