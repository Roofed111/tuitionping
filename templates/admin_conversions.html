What these numbers mean

  • Engaged does not prove human identity or purchase intent. It requires JavaScript plus trusted interaction after 8 visible seconds, scrolling with 30 visible seconds, or server-confirmed account activity. Quiet one-page visitors remain visible separately and are not classified as bots.
  • First-touch source uses a signed first-party cookie that expires after 30 days. Campaign labels use utm_source, utm_medium and utm_campaign. The Visitors source column also shows safe utm_content and utm_term labels and attribution evidence. Advertising markers are recognized without saving their values. Search keywords are aggregate Search Console data and cannot be assigned to a particular visitor.
  • Downloads count successful file requests, not whether a person opened the file.
  • Checkout and trial completion come from live Stripe confirmations. Paid means a positive subscription invoice confirmed by Stripe, including invoices marked paid outside Stripe. Parent PAID replies and demo payments never establish a paid conversion.
  • First tuition reminder means Twilio accepted a real scheduled tuition message; it does not establish delivery or payment.
  • Known Bot and Likely Bot visitors and their conversions are excluded from Human reports. Logged-in browsing, Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control remain excluded from conversion collection. JavaScript confirms browser execution, not human identity.
  • History and browser-to-account links are retained for auditing. Reporting windows and the existing reset boundary change what is displayed, without deleting raw records. Older conversion cookies without enough classification evidence appear in Unknown audit; historical IP hits cannot be safely linked to those cookies.
