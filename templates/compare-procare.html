Common questions

Can TuitionPing replace Procare?

No. Procare runs enrollment, attendance, staff scheduling, parent communication, compliance records and integrated billing. TuitionPing does one of those jobs — scheduled tuition reminder texts and parent payment reports — and deliberately leaves the rest to the tools you already use.

How much does Procare cost?

Procare prices by quote: a flat subscription with no per-child fee, based on the plan and any add-on features you select. It does not publish a simple monthly price list. TuitionPing publishes its prices: ${{ plans.micro.price }}, ${{ plans.starter.price }}, ${{ plans.growth.price }} and ${{ plans.multisite.price }} per month by family and location limits, with a 30-day free trial.

Can I use TuitionPing alongside Procare?

Yes. Keep Procare for operations and payment collection, and use TuitionPing when you specifically want plain text-message reminders that reach parents on any phone — no parent portal or app — with a PAID reply you verify against your records. The two do not sync, so keep one system as your ledger of record.

If I already have Procare, do I need a reminder tool?

Maybe not. Procare's billing workflow includes family accounts and payment posting, and its team can show you its notification options. Add a separate reminder tool only if families still miss due dates because platform notifications are not landing the way a plain text does.
