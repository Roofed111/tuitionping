Daycare tuition collection, from invoice to receipt

A repeatable process for home daycares and small childcare programs. Pick the step you need today, then keep the same record through billing, reminders and payment verification.

Start with a clear record

You need five details for each bill: family or account label, service period, agreed amount, due date, and how to pay. Keep the document number on your payment record so you can connect a receipt to the original invoice.

01 · Agree on the terms

Make the due date clear

Write the amount, billing period, due date, payment method and contact for questions. Fill in any agreed grace period or fee terms. Give families the same written information before a bill is due.

Customize the payment policy template

02 · Create the bill

Show what is being charged

List tuition and any charges you have already assessed. Subtract approved credits and earlier verified payments. A weekly bill and a monthly bill each need their own service period and due date.

Use the free invoice generator

03 · Follow up privately

Send the right reminder

Check the current balance before sending. Start with a friendly due-date message, then follow up on any balance that remains. Use the family's preferred language and leave disputed amounts for a private conversation.

Choose a ready-to-edit reminder · Handle a late-payment conversation

04 · Verify and record

Issue a receipt after checking

Match the amount, date and reference to your payment records. Record each partial payment separately. A PAID message is something to check; a verified receipt records the amount actually received.

Create a verified-payment receipt

05 · Close the billing cycle

Reconcile the remaining balance

Compare your bills and receipts with the payments received. Update the ledger, resolve unmatched payments, and keep documents private. Carry the actual unpaid balance into your next follow-up.

Your end-of-cycle checklist

  • Every bill has an amount, service period, due date and payment method.
  • Every recorded payment has been matched to your records.
  • Each receipt identifies the amount received this time.
  • Unpaid balances, credits and disputed items have clear notes.
  • Saved documents are accessible only to people who need them.
