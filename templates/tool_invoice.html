Invoice or receipt: which should I use?

An invoice asks for payment and shows what remains due. A receipt records an amount you have verified was received. A parent saying “PAID” is a report to check, not proof for issuing a receipt.

How are partial payments handled?

Example: $800 tuition plus a $25 assessed fee, minus a $25 credit, makes an $800 bill. With $200 previously verified and $300 received today, today's receipt shows $300 received and $300 remaining.

Can I use weekly or monthly billing?

Yes. Choose the service period and dates for each document. These manual documents do not change the billing schedules supported by your TuitionPing account.

Are documents stored?

No. Reloading or closing this tab clears the entries. Print or save a PDF before leaving and keep it with your payment records.
