Choosing a tuition follow-up process

Daycare payment spreadsheet vs. automated reminders

A payment spreadsheet gives you a record of bills, credits and verified payments. Scheduled reminder texts handle the repeat follow-up around monthly due dates. You can use both: keep your tracker for reconciliation and use TuitionPing for the messages and parent replies.

Record weekly or monthly bills, partial payments, approved credits and notes in the format that works for you. Our free Excel tracker includes a worked example and Google Sheets import instructions. You enter and verify the records, then send any reminders yourself.

A monthly example: 12 families and one due day

In this fictional program, tuition is due on the 1st. With a spreadsheet, the provider reviews the balances, copies each family's amount into a reminder, sends it privately and records the follow-up. When a family says it paid, the provider checks the payment records and updates the sheet.

With TuitionPing alongside the sheet, the provider enters the family roster separately and sets the monthly reminders. A family receives a text, replies PAID and appears in payment review. The provider checks the actual payment, confirms receipt in TuitionPing and updates the ledger. Verified payment stops that family's scheduled follow-up for the period.

Common questions

Can I keep using Excel or Google Sheets?

Yes. Keep the sheet as your ledger and use TuitionPing for scheduled monthly texts and payment reports. The tools do not automatically sync with each other.

Can I upload the free tracker to TuitionPing?

The tracker is one row per bill. TuitionPing's CSV import uses a separate family-roster format. Follow the import instructions in your account; uploading the tracker is not a supported way to import bill rows.

Will this automate weekly tuition reminders?

The subscription uses monthly due-day schedules. The free tracker, reminder generator and invoice tool can help you manage manually entered weekly dates.

Should I use a broader billing platform instead?

If you need payment collection, autopay, attendance or enrollment in one platform, compare those requirements with a broader system. Our TuitionPing and brightwheel comparison explains the difference in scope.
