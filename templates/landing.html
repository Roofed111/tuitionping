Common questions

Do parents need to install anything?

No. They receive ordinary text messages from your TuitionPing number and reply like any other text. No app, no account, no login.

How long does setup take?

About 10 minutes: create your account, add families (or import a CSV), and you're done. Reminders start going out on the next scheduled stage.

Can a parent opt out?

Yes — they reply STOP anytime and we stop texting them immediately. They can rejoin with START. Opt-outs are honored automatically and instantly.

Do I need parents' consent to text them?

Yes. You should collect consent at enrollment or include it in your tuition agreement. You're responsible for only adding numbers you have permission to text — the signup and import flows both ask you to confirm this.

Can I change what the messages say?

Yes. All four reminder templates are editable from your dashboard, so they sound like you — including your program's name automatically.

What if a parent's number changes or is wrong?

Update it on your dashboard in seconds. A wrong number means a missed reminder, not a penalty — and phone numbers are validated on entry so typos get caught.

What happens after the 30-day trial?

Your card on file is charged for the plan you chose. Cancel anytime from the Billing page — it takes effect at the end of your billing period, and your data stays intact until then.

Is there a contract?

No. Month-to-month or annual, your choice. Cancel in one click whenever you want.

Does TuitionPing collect tuition or verify payments?

Neither. TuitionPing sends the reminders and records it when a parent replies PAID — but it doesn't process payments or confirm the money arrived. You verify payments in whatever system you already use (cash, check, bank transfer, Brightwheel, etc.), and can mark families paid by hand on your dashboard. A parent's PAID reply shows as "Reported paid" until you confirm it.
