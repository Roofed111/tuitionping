What the tracker calculates

FieldWhat it means
Balance dueTuition plus fees assessed by you, minus approved credits and verified payments; minimum zero.
Days overdueCalendar days between the due date and your chosen review date, while a balance remains.
StatusPaid, overdue, due today, or upcoming. No due date means it is not ready for overdue classification.
Parent reported PAIDA separate note. It does not reduce the balance until you verify the payment and enter the amount.

The workbook does not assess late fees automatically or send messages. Use your policy and the tuition late-fee calculator before entering an assessed fee.

How to use it in five minutes

  1. On the Tracker tab, enter the date you are reviewing balances. It stays fixed until you change it.
  2. Create one row per bill, using the billing period and family or account label. For weekly billing, each week gets its own row.
  3. Enter tuition, due date, approved credits, and any fees you have assessed.
  4. Enter only payments you have checked against your payment records in the verified-payment column.
  5. Filter Status to Overdue, record your last follow-up, and use the reminder text templates for your next message.

Keep your completed workbook private. Use account labels instead of children's names where possible, and share access only with staff who need it.

Worked example: a partially paid tuition bill

A $300 weekly bill with a $20 approved credit, a $10 fee assessed by the provider, and $100 in verified payments has a $190 remaining balance. If the review date is October 12 and the due date was October 5, it is 7 days overdue. A parent-reported PAID note alone leaves the $190 unchanged.

Can I use it for monthly preschool tuition?

Yes. The spreadsheet uses the due date on each bill; it does not assume a particular billing frequency. Each bill has one row. Keep a separate payment ledger if you need to reconcile multiple payment transactions within the same bill.

Does this connect to TuitionPing?

No. This is a standalone record. TuitionPing's separate CSV import uses its own family-roster format; this workbook is not an import file. TuitionPing schedules reminder texts, while you remain responsible for verifying payments.
