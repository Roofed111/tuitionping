How to help a provider

  1. Reply and identify the sticking point. Ask how many families they bill, their current payment method, their billing frequency and whether they already have a TuitionPing account. If their billing schedule is not supported, explain that before recommending a trial.
  2. Guide their own account setup. Help them choose the capacity and location plan they need, then let them create their account, enter their card at Stripe checkout, and verify their email. Never ask for a password or card details.
  3. Prepare a CSV with sample rows first. Use the sample CSV available in the wizard’s Families step to explain name, phone, tuition, due_day and optional columns. Keep actual family names and numbers in the provider's own file; they upload it directly after confirming parent SMS permission. Saving a family sends the existing welcome text.
  4. Walk through the four-step wizard. Program details → families → preview → test text. Help check the actual due dates, timezone, sending hours, payment link and messages. Use the provider's own number for the test; keep actual due dates unchanged.
  5. Explain payment review. PAID is a parent's report. The provider checks their payment records and confirms receipt in the dashboard. Reminders and payment processing are separate.
  6. Confirm they can continue independently. Ask whether their test arrived and whether they can review a reported payment. Mark Complete when they confirm they are set up; record an agreed follow-up if they still need help.

Track the conversation with Replied → Helping → Complete, or Not proceeding. This inbox does not enroll a requester in marketing or grant access to their account.
